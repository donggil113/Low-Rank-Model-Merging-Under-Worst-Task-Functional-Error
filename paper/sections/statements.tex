\subsection*{AI use statement}

This draft was produced almost entirely by a generative AI system: Claude, an AI assistant made
by Anthropic, working as a coding and writing agent in a repository session. No human author has
yet reviewed it (status: HUMAN\_REVIEW\_PENDING). The disclosure categories of the ICLR~2027 AI
Policy for Authors apply as follows.

\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{Required disclosures.}
\begin{itemize}
\item The AI system implemented all methods, references and experiment runners.
\item It generated the synthetic data sets.
\item It formulated the hypotheses and designed the diagnostic experiments.
\item It formulated the mathematical statements and wrote their proofs, using standard arguments.
\item It interpreted the results.
\end{itemize}
The research question and the constraints on scope, compute and permitted assets were set by a
human in natural-language instructions.
\item \emph{Recommended disclosures.} The AI system also:
\begin{itemize}
\item drafted every section of this paper;
\item created the tables and the figure;
\item searched for and summarised related literature, partly through AI sub-agents;
\item formatted the references from metadata fetched from arXiv, Crossref and the AAAI
proceedings.
\end{itemize}
\end{itemize}

Verification was also done by the AI system, and is limited to the following:
\begin{itemize}
\item automated unit tests and independent numerical references (Appendix~\ref{app:repro});
\item citation metadata that is machine-extracted rather than typed;
\item a claim--evidence table linking statements to files (\texttt{paper/claim\_evidence.tsv}).
\end{itemize}
Most related-work descriptions rest on summaries by AI sub-agents, and only a few sources were
read directly (see the claim--evidence table). No human has yet checked the proofs, the literature
summaries or the code. Responsibility for the final content lies with the human authors once they
have reviewed it.

\subsection*{Reproducibility statement}

Appendix~\ref{app:repro} lists the exact commands, the commit identifiers, the configuration and
data hashes, and the resource limits of every run. All tables and numbers in the text are
generated from the recorded result files by one exporter script. Appendix~\ref{app:proofs} states
the assumptions and gives complete proofs of the elementary lemmas. Appendix~\ref{app:planned}
lists every experiment that was planned but not run.
